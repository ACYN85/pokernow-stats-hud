# PokerNow Stats HUD 1.6.0 - compatibility release

Prepared for final publication approval; not yet published.
Build: v1.6.0-compatibility-20261030-final.

## Highlights

- **Identity linking:** explicitly group eligible, distinguishable saved accounts
  into a Person, review constituent accounts, and unlink without deleting their
  individual histories. This is a user-managed association, not verified account
  ownership. Ambiguous identities and conflicting/overlapping histories are refused.
- **Unified player statistics:** switch between individual accounts and eligible
  combined Career statistics, calculated from underlying counters rather than
  averaging percentages. Session and Career retain their distinct scopes.
- **Custom assigned names:** use local display labels without changing original
  account identities, notes or statistical attribution.
- **Compatibility and stability:** retain the established V1.5 live ingestion
  engine, HUD, dashboards, notes, tracked players, Settings and legacy Pot Odds.
  Include corrected first-hand detection for qualified waiting-to-blind-posting
  transitions, extension-context retirement with a visible refresh warning, and
  accurate pending-write acknowledgement/recovery reporting.

## Data and update safety

Existing DB5's 13 stores and Backup formats are preserved. No historical hand is
backfilled or reconstructed. Back up before updating; preserve the existing
extension load path and ID. After an extension update, refresh PokerNow between
hands before the next deal. Do not uninstall or clear extension data to update.

Legacy Backup v1/v2 omit Person memberships. Use the explicit Person + Career
backup for associations; that bundle excludes notes and Settings. Career-only
exports do not protect all Session history or preferences. Keep complete backups
private. Runtime rollback does not undo later data writes or imports.

## Known limitations and acceptance scope

- Incomplete hand observation, including refreshing after a deal starts, may
  exclude that hand. Refresh before dealing and allow a complete hand to be
  observed. A hand that never reached durable storage is not recoverable merely
  by refreshing. A durable acknowledged outbox can be replayed with deduplication;
  unacknowledged writes are not promised recoverable.
- The floating Pot Odds widget can appear above PokerNow Session Log/Ledger
  dialogs. This layering issue is deferred to V1.7.
- Genuine native gameplay/account admission and native numerical Pot Odds are
  excluded. All three native production gates remain FALSE; V1.5 is the only
  live gameplay writer. Unsupported data is not certified by synthetic tests.
- Candidate 2 is the validated runtime baseline. Both fixes passed focused
  Node/DOM and disposable Chrome checks. The user confirmed the first hand and
  two completed hands counted in both regular profiles. A later one-hand Test1
  discrepancy is inconclusive because refresh/deal timing is unknown; neither
  its cause nor recovery is established. Between-hands refresh subsequently
  appeared to track correctly.
- Release recommendation remains CONDITIONAL GO, with no confirmed unresolved
  technical blocker. Exact manual VPIP/PFR outcomes, quantitative regular-profile
  persistence across refresh and absence of fresh context/storage errors remain
  unconfirmed. Automated recovery/replay evidence does not turn those manual
  checks into PASS. No real-world success rate or all-variant certification is
  claimed.
